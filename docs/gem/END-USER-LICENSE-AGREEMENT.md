## 4. Nature of the results
1. Scores, similarities, colour names, plate reads and AI descriptions help an officer find and
   review footage. They are not an identification and not evidence by themselves; the original
   recording is.
2. The Section 63 certificate generated by the software is a form filled with what the software
   knows. It becomes a certificate only when the persons named in Part A and Part B have checked
   every particular and signed it. The software never signs or fills in a person's particulars.
3. The enhancement filters show detail present in the recording; they do not add information.
   Every enhanced still carries its filter log and hashes so that it can be reproduced.
